Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Lexiloop collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server. Everything a learner does stays on the device it was typed on.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • The app works with no connection, and makes no external network requests.
  • There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • Progress is stored on the device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

What the app keeps on your device

So that practice survives between sessions, Lexiloop stores the following locally, using the device's own storage for the app:

  • the words practised, and which ones were spelled correctly
  • levels, experience, streaks and achievements
  • the learner names you create for profiles on a shared device
  • your settings: sound, haptics, text size, learning level and theme

This information never leaves the device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to send it to: the app has no backend, no account system and no sign-in.

Audio and word lists

Every word, meaning, example sentence and pronunciation recording is bundled inside the app. Playing a word does not stream or download anything.

Network access

Lexiloop does not contact any server. The Android build declares the internet permission because the framework it is built on requires it, but the app only loads its own files from inside itself.

Permissions

  • Storage — to keep progress and settings on the device.
  • Vibration — for the optional haptic feedback on the keypad.

No permission is used to read your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or files, and the app never asks for them.

Children's privacy

Lexiloop is designed for children and used in families and classrooms. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is no children's data to collect, share or delete, and nothing is directed to advertising or profiling. A parent or teacher can remove everything by uninstalling the app, or by clearing its storage in the device settings.

Purchases and advertising

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising identifiers.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The current version of this policy always applies to the current version of the app.
